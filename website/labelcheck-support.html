LabelCheck Support

LabelCheck scans any packaged food and tells you what is in it in 2 seconds, privately. Point the camera at a barcode, or photograph the ingredients panel. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your scan history, favorites, and allergen profiles stay on your phone.

Restore purchases

Pro is unlocked through Apple. To restore it on a new device, or after reinstalling:

  1. Open LabelCheck.
  2. Go to Settings, then Get LabelCheck Pro.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro.

Your purchase status is checked with Apple each time the app opens. If restore does not work, check that you are signed in with the correct Apple ID in iOS Settings, then try again.

Cancel a subscription

LabelCheck cannot cancel a subscription for you, because the transaction is with Apple. To cancel:

  1. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  2. Find LabelCheck Pro and tap Cancel Subscription.
  3. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current paid period.

To request a refund, use Apple Support or reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles refunds, not LabelCheck.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign in, and no personal details are requested.

Does LabelCheck work offline?

Partly. Your scan history, allergen profiles, and reading a label from a photo all work offline, because they happen on your phone. Re-scanning a product you have scanned before also works offline, because the app keeps a copy of that product on your phone. Looking up a barcode for the first time needs an internet connection, because the product data comes from Open Food Facts.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes your scan history, favorites, and allergen profiles stored on your device. There is no server copy to recover. Use the history export feature in Pro before deleting if you want to keep a copy.

The app says a product contains an allergen. Can I trust it?

Treat the physical label as the answer. Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a crowdsourced open database, and it can be incomplete or out of date. Label reading from a photo is a reading aid. LabelCheck is general information only: not medical or dietary advice, and not an allergy safety guarantee.

Why is a product not found?

Open Food Facts does not have every product. If a barcode is not found, photograph the ingredients panel instead, search by product name (Pro), or add the product to Open Food Facts so the next person finds it.

Is LabelCheck medical or dietary advice?

No. LabelCheck is a general information tool. It is not medical or dietary advice, and it is not a substitute for professional help, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any medical condition.

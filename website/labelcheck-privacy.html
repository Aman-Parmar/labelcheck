LabelCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

LabelCheck is published by Aman Parmar. Contact: support@singhsec.com.

Summary

LabelCheck collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your scan history, favorites, and allergen profiles stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is the barcode you scan, sent to Open Food Facts to fetch that product's information, and, on a product with Nutri-Score D or E, that product's category, sent to Open Food Facts to fetch same-category products with a higher Nutri-Score. With Pro, the words you type when you search by product name are also sent to Open Food Facts to find matching products.

What the app does with your data

  • Barcodes you scan. When you scan or type a barcode, the app sends that barcode, and only that barcode, to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. Open Food Facts is an independent open database; its own privacy policy applies to that lookup. No name, email, location, or device identifier is sent with it.
  • Product categories for alternatives. On a product card with Nutri-Score D or E, the app sends that product's category (for example, "chocolate spreads") to Open Food Facts to fetch same-category products with a higher Nutri-Score. Only the category is sent. No name, email, location, or device identifier is sent with it.
  • Search words (Pro). When you search by product name, the app sends the words you typed to Open Food Facts to find matching products. Only those words are sent. No name, email, location, or device identifier is sent with them.
  • Camera and photos. The camera is used to scan barcodes, and a photo you choose is used to read a label. Images are processed on your phone (Apple Vision and Natural Language frameworks) and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Scan history, favorites, and allergen profiles. Stored on your phone only, in the app's local database. They are never synced, shared, or uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Purchases. Pro purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app never sees your card number or payment details. Apple may share a transaction record with us through App Store Connect for accounting; we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, locations, contacts, identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising data. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Product data

Product information (names, Nutri-Score, NOVA group, allergens, ingredients, nutrition) comes from Open Food Facts, a crowdsourced open database licensed under the ODbL. It may be incomplete or out of date. LabelCheck shows it as general information only: it is not medical or dietary advice, and it is not an allergy safety guarantee. Always check the physical label, and check with a doctor about dietary questions.

Children

LabelCheck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children. Because the app collects nothing at all, there is nothing to delete on request, but you can contact us at the address above with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://labelcheck.singhsec.com/labelcheck-privacy with a new "last updated" date. Because the app collects no contact details, we cannot notify you directly; check this page before updating the app if it matters to you.
